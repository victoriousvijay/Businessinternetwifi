Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have. By using the website you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our quote, availability, call-back or contact forms, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, ZIP or postal code, street address, the plan or service you are interested in and any message you write.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the date and time of each request. We also store your light or dark theme choice in your browser so the site remembers it.

We do not knowingly collect payment card numbers, government ID numbers or passwords through this website.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, call or email you back and prepare a quote.
  • To check service availability at your address with carriers and partners and help arrange installation.
  • To send you confirmations and follow-ups about your request.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website and to prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations and to establish or defend legal claims.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed:

  • Carriers, installers and partners when required to check availability, place an order or schedule installation that you asked for.
  • Service providers that help us run the website, such as hosting, email delivery and font delivery. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities or advisers when the law requires it or to protect our rights, customers or the public.
  • A successor if our business is merged, sold or reorganised, subject to this policy.

4. Calls, texts and email

When you submit your phone number or email address you agree that we may contact you about your request, including by phone and email. Message and data rates may apply if we text you. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying STOP to a text, using the unsubscribe link in an email or contacting us using the details below.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only the browser storage needed for the site to work and to remember your theme choice. We do not use advertising cookies. Third-party services we load, such as Google Fonts, may receive your IP address when fonts are delivered.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to handle your request and to meet legal, accounting and dispute-resolution needs. Enquiries that do not lead to an order are normally deleted within 24 months.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to enquiry data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive a copy of it and to opt out of marketing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and we will respond within the time required by law, generally within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

9. Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Links to other websites

Our website may link to carriers or other third-party sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised policy.
